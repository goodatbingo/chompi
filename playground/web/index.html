What runs here

The page loads tape_fx.wasm into an AudioWorklet. Per sample it runs the first loop of DSPEngine::ApplyFx(): DC block, DJ filter, soft-clip saturation with gain compensation, then warble. Knob values go through the firmware's own mappings, and the LEDs use the panel's colour fades. Delay and reverb are not included yet.

About

An unofficial community tool built from the MIT-licensed open-source firmware release. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the hardware's makers.
